# Sign-off, post-PVT set — all-hv, replica-biased cells

**KIND: SIGN-OFF (set 2).** Two sizings (each in an ideal-cap and a PDK-MIM-cap realisation) of the **same signal-path topology as
[`pre-pvt/`](../pre-pvt/)** (branch-stacked SSF, bridge and gm_f merge intact,
floating differential caps), re-issued as `lab.dut` topology **`d`**: the two
bridge gates take a rail `vbr` generated by one shared three-device replica
branch (diode copies of `gmf_b` and `bridge`, sunk by a mirror unit) instead of
the n-mirror rail. Nothing in the signal path moves; the ladder current becomes
mirror-referenced instead of threshold-referenced. Full derivation, screens and
every intermediate cell: [`experiments/023-replica-bias/README.md`](../../experiments/023-replica-bias/README.md).

What changed versus `pre-pvt/`, and why this set exists:

* **No lv devices.** VDD = 1.5 V is above the lv (1.2 V) rating; eight of the
  nine `pre-pvt` cells used lv `in_a` and/or lv `gmf_b`. Both cells here are
  `sg13_hv_*` only. The lv `in_a` was the input-CM lever, so the input CM sits
  at **0.22–0.24 V** (see *Assumptions*).
* **Every S1–S8 line passes at nominal with margin**, and every one of the four
  process corners (`ss/ff/sf/fs`) passes at 27 °C / 1.5 V — `pre-pvt` cells
  spanned 3–38× in fc across process.
* PVT is reported as a **measured envelope**, not a box (the reviewer's
  expectation that a nA-class filter does not meet a full PVT box holds; the
  mechanism is in 023 §5).

Both cells are **layout-legal** (`lab.grid.legalize` + `restore_fc`, asserted at
packaging), their certified decks are in `<cell>/asbuilt/`, and **both identity
gates pass** on each (`scripts/draw_xschem.py check` / `sim`, native xschem
lane): the drawn `lpf_core_H*.sch` netlists to the as-built subckt with a full
net bijection (15 MOS + 6 caps, `vbr`/`rep_x` included), and simulating the
drawing's own testbench sheets reproduces every scorecard line and THD to
≤ 5e-4. `draw_xschem.py` learned topology `d` for this: the replica branch is
drawn as one column right of biquad B under the same vdd rail, and the two
bridge-gate labels read `vbr`; nothing else in the picture moved. Op
annotations (`annotate_op.py`) are stamped on each core sheet.

## The four cells

Two sizings, each in two capacitor realisations. **Device sizes are rounded to
natural values** (integer µm, 0.5 µm where a device is small; fingers ≤ 10 µm)
and the caps re-fitted (0.01 pF trim); the `-pdk-cap` cells then re-fit the
same sizing through the **PDK MIM model** (`cap_cmim`, `cornerCAP.lib
cap_typ`, 1.5 fF/µm² + 40 aF/µm perimeter): every capacitor is `m` identical
square units of side ≤ 50 µm (`lab.dut.cmim_geom`), the design farads realised
to 10 nm of side.

## Robustness (measured; `experiments/023-replica-bias/*.robust.a1p1.json`, `tsw_*`, `vsw_*`, `round_*.log`)

Bias law for the temperature rows: `LPF_BIAS_ALPHA=1.1`, i.e. the ideal
reference current scales as (T/300 K)^1.1 — a model of a constant-gm-class
reference; α = 0 (constant current) gives fc ∝ 1/T and passes at 27 °C only.
MIM caps sit at `cap_typ` on the P/V/T grid (`cap_typ_mismatch` in MC); the
±10 % `cap_bcs/wcs` corners are not swept.

| | `H5-lean` / `H5-pdk-cap` | `H12-robust` / `H12-pdk-cap` | `pre-pvt` best (`B-balanced` / family) |
|---|---|---|---|
| process ss/ff/sf/fs @ 27 °C, 1.5 V | **4/4 PASS**, fc 247.2–252.6 (1.02×) | **4/4 PASS**, fc 247.4–252.6 (1.02×) | 3–38× fc span, fails |
| supply @ 27 °C, every line | **1.35–1.65 V** (all nine steps) | **1.40–1.65 V** (1.35: dc −0.78, ph 308) | 1.5 V only |
| temperature @ 1.5 V, every line | **−20 … +55 °C** | **0 … +70 °C** | 27 °C only |
| one-axis set (9) / reduced 22 / full 45 | 7/9 · 8/22 · 18/45 | 6/9 · 6/22 · 16/45 | —/1/— (1/22) |
| **MC all-pass yield** (n = 100, tt mismatch, 27 °C) | **67 % / 64 %** (σ fc 4.9 Hz; S1 phase 99 %) | **82 % / 82 %** (σ fc 3.7 Hz; S1 phase 100 %) | 70 % (B) / 45–96 % |

Every failing grid corner of either cell contains 1.35 V, −40/−20 °C or
≥ 85 °C, and is a **headroom** failure of the merged ladder (one device leaves
saturation), never a bias failure — fc over the passing region stays within
±1 %. The temperature window is narrower than the lv-`in_a` cells of 023
(`B1-y2`: −40…+70 °C) because the hv `in_a`'s |V_SG| drifts with the rest of
the ladder; the mechanism and the reason no vicm fixes it are in 023 §5.
Rounding the devices cost nothing measurable (H5's phase and 1.35 V margin
improved: the 20/21 µm bias unit at ng 2); the MIM model costs 0.3 dB of THD on
H12 and 3 % of MC yield on H5, i.e. the ideal-cap numbers were honest.

## Which to continue with

**`H12-pdk-cap`** — the sizing with margin on every axis that matters for a
paper claim (THD −50.4 = 10.4 dB, IRN 29.2 = 10.8 µV, phase 332.4 with 100 %
S1 yield in MC, all process corners, 82 % yield, for 11.9 nW and 184 pF), in
its PDK-capacitor realisation: it is the cell to take to layout. `H12-robust`
is the same sizing with ideal caps (the pre-layout reference for it);
`H5-lean` / `H5-pdk-cap` are the low-power / low-area marker of the same
design (8.9 nW, 142 pF) — wider supply window (1.35–1.65 V) but 64–67 % yield.

## Assumptions

* **Input CM = 0.22–0.24 V** is a property of the all-hv stacked ladder at
  1.5 V (bottom constraint `vicm + |V_SG|(in_a) ≥ V_GS(gmf_a) + 4kT/q`); the
  preceding stage must provide it. `vocm` ≈ 1.25–1.28 V.
* The bias reference is ideal in the bench (`lab.deck._bias`); "α = 1.1" is a
  model of what a constant-gm reference would deliver, not a circuit here.
* THD is a nominal, single-sample number (coherent strobed transient, S7
  definition); it is not Monte-Carlo'd.
