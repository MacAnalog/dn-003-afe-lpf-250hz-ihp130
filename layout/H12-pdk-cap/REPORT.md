### What the PEX netlist does and does not contain

kpex 2.5D cannot run on a layout containing `cap_cmim` (see §8), so the
extraction GDS is the **MIM-stripped copy** produced by the generator itself
(`gen_H12_pdk_cap.py --pex-gds …`: clears MIM 36/0 and Vmim 129/0 and removes
the TopMetal1 *plates*, keeping the TopMetal1 hauls and via-stack pads), and the
six `cap_cmim` cards are re-inserted verbatim from the certified netlist
(kpex deletes every capacitor *device* on principle — "whiteboxed").

* **included**: every Metal1/2/3/5, TopMetal1, poly and via parasitic of the
  routing; the **Metal5 bottom-plate capacitance to substrate** — the number the
  BRIEF §6 flip exists to place (`voutp` 493 fF, `voutn` 347 fF, `vout_1`
  218 fF, all on high-budget nets); the MOS junction areas (`AS/AD/PS/PD` from
  the layout, so the model's `pre_layout` estimate is bypassed).
* **not included**: capacitance from the MIM **top** plates to their
  neighbourhood. In silicon that plate is screened from below by its own bottom
  plate (0.04 µm away) and sees nothing above, so the omission is small; the
  cross-check in §8 quantifies it as ≤ 15 fF on `net2`/`net3` and ≤ 91 fF on
  `net4`/`net1`, and both of those cross-check numbers are themselves an
  over-count (the tool does not apply the bottom plate's shielding).

* **The headline constraint is met exactly.** The A→B feedback path the brief
  calls "a topology hazard, not a budget" (§1.1, §10) has **no extraction
  element at all**: `net2`/`net3` never leave island A, the two cap banks are
  ~300 µm and one grounded shield band apart, and no biquad-B net is routed into
  island A on any layer. The reviewer's one number — C(`net2`,`net4`) +
  C(`net2`,`voutp`) + C(`net2`,`net1`) + C(`net2`,`voutn`) — is **0.000 fF**
  against a 0.28 fF allowance.
* **`net2`/`net3` are 2.2× over the *balanced* budget** and this is the cell's
  one real layout cost: 50.0 / 44.9 fF against 22.8 fF, worth the measured
  1.12° of S1. The dominant term is `VSUBS` (36.5 / 32.7 fF), and inside it the
  TopMetal1 haul that carries `net2` from the `xc13` top plate to island A:
  ≈217 µm of run at 37.4 aF/µm of *perimeter* fringe ≈ 16 fF, plus the Metal2
  lane and the Metal1 straps. It is perimeter, not area — a narrower or
  lower-metal haul buys almost nothing (Metal2 fringe is 34.8 aF/µm). The lever
  that does work is **length**, i.e. moving the `xc13`/`xc17` sub-arrays from the
  ends of bank A to the axis (PLAN §3 currently puts `xc19` on the axis). That is
  a floorplan change and therefore a new PLAN gate — see Next Steps.
* The **one-sided** numbers are all comfortable: 5.1 fF of `net2`/`net3`
  asymmetry against 45.7 fF, 10 fF of `net4`/`net1` against 166 fF. The large
  `vout_1`−`vout_2` (156 fF) and `voutp`−`voutn` (153 fF) differences are the
  deliberate single-orientation plate assignments of `xc19` and `xc12`
  (PLAN §2 deviation, BRIEF §6 "OK but asymmetric"): they land 218 fF and
  493 fF of bottom plate on one half only, against 6710 fF and 1800 fF
  one-sided budgets — 12–43× of margin, and the S1/S2 cost is already in the
  measured scorecard.
* `net4`/`net1` at 77 / 67 fF sit **under** the nominal 82.8 fF budget but over
  the 34.8 fF pvt budget; their contribution (0.50°) is already counted in S1.

**Corner caveat, stated plainly.** The nominal S1 margin after layout is 0.73°.
The pre-layout *worst passing corner* had 1.00° of `ph_max` margin
(`PRELAYOUT.md` §2/§5), and the layout costs 1.65°, so **S1 is expected to fail
at that corner**. Nothing in this report re-runs the corner set — the campaign's
post-layout corner sweep is the next gate, and the two levers are the `net2`
haul length above and the `iref` trim (which moves `fc`, not phase).

Two DRC floors are enforced **inside** the generator regardless of the knob, so
no legal knob value can produce a violation: the same-well pmos pitch is
`max(dev_gap_x, 2·well_margin + 0.9)` (TGO.e = 0.86 µm ThickGateOx space), and
tap Activ bars stop `ring_w + 0.7 µm` short of a crossing bar (pSD.b = 0.31).
`W`, `L`, `ng`, `m` are read from `design.json` and are not knobs; the cap unit
sizes are transcribed from `core.sp` and cross-checked against
`design.json["caps_pf"]` at build time (assertion, 2 % tolerance).

Determinism: `build()` takes no wall clock, no randomness and no absolute path;
two rebuilds give the identical sha256 (`e5cfa1da…86a9c`).

## 9. Gotchas worth remembering

* **kpex 2.5D cannot extract a `cap_cmim`.** `klayout_pex_protobuf/ihp-sg13g2_tech.pb.json`
  gives the MIM top layer (`cmim_top`, GDS 36/0) `original_layer_name: "<TODO>"`,
  and the 2.5D engine dies with `RuntimeError: KeyError: '<TODO>' in
  EdgeNeighborhoodVisitor.on_edge` (`sidewall_and_fringe_extractor.py:383`).
  Two ways out were tried:
  1. **used here** — strip the MIM from the extraction GDS
     (`gen_H12_pdk_cap.py --pex-gds`, `--pex-lvs`) and re-insert the six
     certified cap cards. Runs on **stock kpex** through the platform wrapper;
     12 s; the bottom-plate parasitic survives.
  2. *recorded, not used* — patch a copy of the tech (call the layer `MIM`, give
     it ~zero specs so it only shields) and drive kpex through a monkeypatched
     `PDK.config`. It also needs the overlap/side-overlap dicts wrapped
     (missing pairs `KeyError` instead of defaulting to 0) and a non-zero
     epsilon (kpex divides by `atan(α·halo)`). It keeps the top plates, but the
     result *over*-counts: the top plate then shows 91 fF (`net4`) and 15 fF
     (`net2`) to substrate that the bottom plate 0.04 µm beneath it screens in
     silicon. Numbers in `/…/tmp/design/signoff/pex_cc/` for whoever fixes
     klayout-pex upstream.
* kpex deletes **every capacitor device** it finds ("Removing whiteboxed
  device", `rcx25/netlist_expander.py:59`); `--blackbox true` keeps them but then
  the SPICE writer aborts on the custom MIM device class
  (`Invalid parameter name: 'C'`). Re-inserting the cards is the only route.
* kpex promotes every *labelled* net to a subckt pin, so the PEX subckt header
  must be rewritten verbatim to the certified pin list (and `vss`/`VSUBS`
  renamed to `0`, dropping the resulting `0`-to-`0` element).
* `ihp.cells.via_stack` draws **0.26 µm** vias; `V1.a`/`Vn.a` require *exactly*
  0.19 µm (and TopVia1 exactly 0.42) — draw the stack yourself.
* `ptap1`/`ntap1` place their contacts over only ~⅔ of the Activ length, which
  trips `LU.d`/`LU.d1` on any bar longer than ~20 µm.
* `_mos_core`'s gate-contact bar spans *across* the inner source/drain columns:
  anything that walks vertically past `y_gate` at an inner column shorts the gate
  rail. This is the sharper form of the de-risk note's rule 2.
* gdsfactory wires must be cap-extended at both ends, or every L-join leaves a
  sub-minimum-width notch (`M1.a`, `Mn.a`) that is invisible in a plot.
* The MIM units of one array must be merged into **one solid Metal5 sheet**: at
  `cap_gap` = 0.4 µm the plate-to-plate space fails `Mn.f` (0.60 µm for shapes
  wider than 10 µm with a >10 µm parallel run) unless the gap is filled.

## Summary

**What was done**

* `gen_H12_pdk_cap.py` — the layout of record for `lpf_core` as one deterministic
  gdsfactory module: 15 certified devices + 8 dummies + 51 MIM units, 8 n-well
  islands with their own taps, two guard rings and a grounded A/B shield band,
  all placement/routing driven by the 24 `LayoutParams` knobs of PLAN §5 and all
  device geometry read from `design.json`.
* Built exactly the approved floorplan: biquad A and biquad B as separate
  islands with the two MIM banks at opposite ends, the 2-D common-centroid bias
  array with a dummy column per side, the `vdd`-well 1-D centroid for
  `xm14`/`xr1`/`xm15` + 2 dummies, and the BRIEF §6 plate flip on `xc1`/`xc10`.
* **DRC 0 violations, no waivers. LVS matches** the certified netlist (via the
  PLAN §7 reference the generator emits). GDS reproduces byte-identically.
* PEX (kpex 2.5D, CC and RC) → `asbuilt/core_pex.sp`, re-measured through the
  block's own frozen benches: **S1–S7 all pass** post-layout;
  `scorecard_post.json` next to the pre-layout scorecard, every delta explained
  and confirmed by a delete-the-parasitic what-if.
* The one number the brief asked for: **C(`net2`, biquad-B) = 0.000 fF** against
  a 0.28 fF budget.

**Assumptions**

* The `--no_density` DRC policy, no fill/sealring/pads/ESD, and no decoupling —
  all from PLAN §6, which the human approved.
* The p-substrate net is called `vss` in the layout and `0` in the schematic;
  the LVS reference and the PEX splice rename accordingly.
* `vbp` stays a dangling labelled pad (BRIEF §9); it therefore has no extracted
  net, and the post-layout subckt keeps it as an unconnected pin exactly as the
  pre-layout netlist does.
* The MIM devices' *value* comes from the certified schematic and only their
  plate parasitics come from PEX (forced by the kpex gap in §9); the top-plate
  environment capacitance is treated as negligible, with the over-counting
  cross-check quoted in §4.
* Nominal-corner measurement only: the post-layout corner/MC sweeps are not part
  of this hand-off.

**Errors / setbacks / gotchas**

* Everything in §9 — the kpex MIM gap and its two workarounds, kpex deleting
  capacitor devices, the 0.26 µm `via_stack` vias, the `ptap1` contact coverage,
  the gate-bar short mechanism, the L-corner slivers, and the merged Metal5
  sheet requirement.
* Two LVS shorts were pure floorplan-blindness rather than DRC problems (a via
  stack landing on the island-A guard ring; a TopMetal1 haul crossing a cap's

* The S1 phase certificate is the one line the layout genuinely spends: 1.65° of
  2.38°, of which 1.12° is `net2`/`net3` ground capacitance dominated by the
  perimeter fringe of the TopMetal1 haul out of the `xc13`/`xc17` bank.
